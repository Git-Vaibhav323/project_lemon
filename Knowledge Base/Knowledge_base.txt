General & About Us
Q: What is Plantify (Planto)?
A: Plantify is a digital platform designed to simplify gardening and plant care. We help users select the perfect plants for their unique space, shop for gardening accessories, access expert care tips, and connect with a community of fellow plant lovers—all while promoting eco-friendly, sustainable habits.

Q: What are the core values of Plantify?
A: Our platform is built on four core pillars:

Sustainability: Reducing environmental impact and protecting biodiversity.

Accessibility: Making gardening easy and achievable for everyone.

Community: Bringing plant enthusiasts together to collaborate and share knowledge.

Education: Providing expert-backed insights on horticulture and nature.

Services & Features
Q: How does the Plant Recommendation feature work?
A: We take the guesswork out of gardening. Plantify analyzes your specific location, environmental constraints, and personal preferences to recommend a curated list of plants—whether you need indoor houseplants, outdoor landscaping, ornamental flowers, or edible crops.

Q: What is Environmental Analysis and how do I use it?
A: This feature evaluates your local surroundings to identify potential ecological risks and growth opportunities. It monitors shifting environmental conditions over time, keeping your garden safe and helping you make data-driven decisions for sustainable land management.

Q: What does the Air Quality Index (AQI) feature do?
A: Our AQI tool reports daily local air quality levels and highlights potential health risks from pollution. It tracks key pollutants, including particulate matter (PM2.5/PM10), ozone, nitrogen dioxide, and sulfur dioxide, so you know exactly what kind of environment your plants (and you!) are breathing in.

E-Commerce & Care
Q: What can I buy in the Plant Store?
A: Our store is a convenient, one-stop shop for everything green. We offer a diverse collection of indoor and outdoor plants, succulents, trees, shrubs, seasonal blooms, and high-quality gardening supplies/accessories delivered directly to your door.

Q: Where do your Gardening Tips come from?
A: Our comprehensive care guides cover everything from soil preparation and watering schedules to pest control and harvesting. This advice is compiled from certified experts, including master gardeners and professional horticulturists, to give your plants the best chance to thrive.

Q: How does Plantify help save time and money?
A: By using our tailored environmental matching tools, you avoid the frustrating trial-and-error of buying plants that aren't suited for your specific environment. This protects your wallet from accidental plant loss and ensures a thriving, cost-effective garden.

Community & Sustainability
Q: Why does Plantify emphasize native plants?
A: Native plants are naturally adapted to your local climate and soil. By encouraging their growth and minimizing reliance on chemical fertilizers or pesticides, we help you cultivate beautiful spaces that naturally support local wildlife, pollinators, and biodiversity.

Q: What is the Plantify Community?
A: It is a collaborative space for users sharing a passion for nature. Members can interact, exchange tips, seek trouble-shooting advice, and work together on local green initiatives to improve regional environmental health and quality of life.
